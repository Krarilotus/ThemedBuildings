# Themed Buildings

**Author**: Monsterfish
**Version**: 1.0.0

Chapels, churches and cathedrals take the look of their owner's lord. European lords build a
chapel, a church and a cathedral; Arabian lords build a small mosque, a mosque and a grand
mosque.

Each building has two pictures: the one in the game files and a themed one this module brings.
In the settings you say which side the game files' picture belongs to (by default the chapel
and cathedral are European and the church is a mosque, as in Reconquista). Every player of the
other side gets the themed picture.

The lord's side is the game's own: the AI lords keep theirs (custom AIs from aiSwapper too),
and you get the side of the lord you play. Nothing is saved in maps or saves.

Needs gmResourceModifier.
